## Licensing boundary

The public repository remains **Apache-2.0**. Apache-2.0 permits Cloudjet to
modify and distribute the upstream work while requiring preservation of the
applicable license and notices. It also permits a distributor to add its own
copyright statement for its own modifications, but that does not transfer
ownership of upstream code.

Accordingly:

1. The root `LICENSE` is intentionally kept intact rather than replacing
   Z.AI's inherited copyright notice with Cloudjet's name.
2. Cloudjet's identity belongs in project metadata, README, About/Credits,
   NOTICE, and this rights map.
3. Cloudjet copyright should be asserted only for work Cloudjet authored or
   validly acquired.
4. If founder, employee, contractor, or contributor rights are later assigned
   to Cloudjet, the repository may update this rights map based on that actual
   chain-of-title evidence. The assignment itself belongs in corporate records,
   not in the public repository.
